- The audit sink now refuses a redirected or blocking object at its path instead of writing into it or hanging: a hard link (an inode another name owns and can read), a pre-created sink whose mode admits group or other accounts, another account's file, and anything that is not a regular file are all refused by an fstat verdict on the opened descriptor, degrading open with an arm-scoped recovery (`chmod 600` where the mode alone failed); a FIFO no longer hangs the extension factory at load — a reader-less one is refused at the open (`O_NONBLOCK`), one with a reader by the verdict.

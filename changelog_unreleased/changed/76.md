- Renamed the project namespace from `ghjig` to `gitjig`: per-clone state now lives at `.gitjig/`, the environment prefix is `GITJIG_*`, and the tier-1 extension loads from `.pi/extensions/gitjig/`. An already-bound clone keeps its hooks binding and needs no re-arm — only its state container moves, and the leftover `.ghjig/` becomes visible to `git status` for removal. (#76)
